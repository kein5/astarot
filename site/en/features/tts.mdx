# Text to speech (TTS)

Speaks your text or its translation into VRChat's microphone, through a virtual microphone. The settings are under Settings → "Text to speech (TTS)".

You can turn speech on and off separately from sending text to the chatbox.

A new installation starts with "Basic" under "Used for", which hides the switch and the settings page of text to speech. Show them with "Other features (N)" on the main screen, or choose a use under "Used for" at the top of the settings menu. Hidden pages can also be opened from the settings search ([First setup](../guide/first-setup)).

## What you need

- A speech model, run by the [speech.cpp](https://github.com/nyosegawa/speech.cpp) servers that come with the app. Japanese is spoken with Irodori-TTS and other languages with Qwen3-TTS. Neither model comes with the app: download one, or point to a file you have ([Model downloads](../guide/models), [Use your own models](../guide/custom-models#tts)). Japanese can also come from VOICEVOX or another engine you install instead ([Read Japanese with VOICEVOX or another engine](#read-japanese-with-voicevox-or-another-engine)).
- A virtual microphone, for VRChat to hear the speech. On Windows, install a virtual cable ([Windows audio route](../guide/windows-audio)). On Linux you can use "Managed virtual microphone (Linux)" ([Linux audio route](../guide/linux-audio)).

## Steps

### First steps

1. Under Settings → "Text to speech (TTS)" → "Models and servers", download a model with "Download a default model", or enter the path of a <Term tip="A file format that holds an AI model in one file. speech.cpp reads it; the file name ends in .gguf.">GGUF</Term> file you have.
2. Settings save by themselves: a typed path when you press Enter or leave the field. Then press "Start server". Saving alone does not load the model.

   ![Settings → Text to speech (TTS)](/screenshots/en/tts.webp)

3. Set "Chat voice" to "Original text". To speak translations, choose "Translated text", and under "Translation slot" choose which target language's translation is spoken.
4. Under "Output device", choose where the speech plays. Try it with your own earphones first. If a device is missing, press "Refresh devices".
5. Go back to the main screen and turn on "Text to speech (TTS)". Text you send is now spoken.

Japanese and the other languages start on the bundled server. With a bundled server selected, turning it on from the main screen also starts the server. To use a server you started yourself, choose "External server" and enter its URL under "External servers and engines" lower on the page. An update from an earlier version keeps the server you used.

The [first setup](../guide/first-setup) screen can also choose and download the speech model.

### Read Japanese with VOICEVOX or another engine

Japanese can also be read in the voices of a speech engine you install and run yourself: VOICEVOX, COEIROINK, AivisSpeech or SHAREVOX. The app does not install or start the engine.

1. Install the engine and start it.
2. Under Settings → "Text to speech (TTS)" → "Japanese TTS", choose the engine in "Engine that reads Japanese".
3. The engine's fields appear under "External servers and engines" lower on the page. When it says "Connected", choose a voice in "Voice". If it says "Not connected", check that the engine is running and check "Engine URL", then press "Check again". The engine's default URL usually works. Only addresses that point to the PC that runs ASTAROT (`127.0.0.1`, `localhost`, `::1`) can be used.
4. Your choices are saved at once. Turn on "Text to speech (TTS)" on the main screen.

For VOICEVOX, AivisSpeech and SHAREVOX, "How (engine) reads" sets the speed, volume, pitch and intonation; for COEIROINK, "How COEIROINK reads" sets the speed, volume and pitch. Saved values apply from the next message read.

While an engine is chosen, Japanese speech uses neither the speech.cpp server nor its model. Other languages are read by speech.cpp as before.

::: warning Voice terms and credits
Each voice has its own terms of use. In videos or streams, credit the voice as its terms say (for example `VOICEVOX:ずんだもん`). COEIROINK voices need the credit "COEIROINK:voice name".
:::

### Start speaking sooner (Japanese, GPU)

"Low-latency standby (Irodori-TTS on GPU)" shortens the time before speech starts. It is off by default.

While nothing is being spoken, it keeps running short calculations on the GPU so that speech can start right away. That uses more power and can compete with the game for the GPU.

### Stop

There are three ways to stop, each with a different scope. "Stop speech" and "Mute" are always at the top of the main screen.

| Action | What stops |
|---|---|
| Turn off "Text to speech (TTS)" on the main screen | New speech, the speech playing now and the speech waiting its turn. Text sending, the voice changer and translation continue |
| "Stop speech" | The speech playing now, the speech waiting its turn, a voice sample and the virtual microphone's test tone. With nothing to stop, it cannot be pressed. Pressed, it shows "Stopping…" and then "Stopped". "Stop speech" under Settings → "Text to speech (TTS)" does the same |
| "Mute" | What ASTAROT sends to VRChat: with the managed virtual microphone, both speech and the voice changer; with an external virtual cable, speech only. It lasts until you unmute, and the button shows "Muted" meanwhile. Press it again to unmute. "Mute now" and "Unmute" under Settings → "Device" → "More settings" → "Audio route" do the same |

All can be assigned to [hotkeys](./quick-chat#hotkeys). After a restart, speech and the bundled servers start off.

## Check that it works

Play a sample to hear the voice. If you want others to hear it in VRChat, also check the virtual microphone in VRChat.

### Hear it yourself

Under "More settings" → "Monitor", turn on "Hear it on my speakers" and choose a "Monitor output" to play the same speech that goes to VRChat on your own speakers or headphones.

- What VRChat receives does not change.
- Use headphones, so your microphone does not pick up the sound from your speakers.

### Play a sample of the voice

You can hear the voice of the model in use on your own, without turning speech on. After changing the voice or the seed, play it again to compare.

1. Under "More settings" → "Monitor", turn on "Hear it on my speakers" and choose a "Monitor output".
2. Under "Models and servers", start the server of the model you use.
3. On the card of the model in use, press "Play sample". A short fixed sentence is read and played only on the monitor output. Japanese models read a Japanese sentence; the other models read an English one.

- The sample is never sent to VRChat. It never plays on the speech output device or on a virtual cable.
- While it plays, "Stop sample" stops it. "Stop speech" and "Mute" stop it too. No sample plays while muted.
- Only the model in use can play a sample. The other cards say why they cannot.
- Voice changer (RVC) models play a sample with "Play sample" on their cards on the "Voice changer (RVC)" page ([Voice changer](./voice-conversion)).

## Troubleshooting

### When it does not work

- If VRChat does not hear it, see [Troubleshooting](../guide/troubleshooting#vrchat-does-not-hear-the-speech).
- If the same text is spoken twice, turn off the other tool that reads the same chat, such as VRCT-TTS by upstream VRCT's author.
- If a model you brought does not work, see [Use your own models](../guide/custom-models#tts).

See [Troubleshooting](../guide/troubleshooting) for fixes by symptom.
