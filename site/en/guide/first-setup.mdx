# First setup

This page sets up translating text and sending it to the VRChat chatbox. Set up speech or captions afterwards, following the page for each feature.

![The ASTAROT main screen with Translation on and a conversation in the log](/screenshots/en/main.webp)

The switches and languages are on the left, the conversation log is on the right, and the message box is at the bottom.

<StepProgress />

## 1. Choose whether to send usage data

On the first start, a screen asks whether to send usage data. Choose "Send", "Don't send" or "Decide later". The app works the same whichever you choose. [Privacy](./privacy) lists what is sent.

## 2. Turn on OSC in VRChat

ASTAROT sends text to the VRChat chatbox over OSC. In VRChat, open the Action Menu, go to "Options" → "OSC" and choose "Enabled".

"Quick settings" at the top right of the main screen says whether a VRChat with OSC enabled was found on your PC. The top of Settings → "VRChat" says the same.

!["Quick settings" open, saying that VRChat was found](/screenshots/en/quick-settings.webp)

By default ASTAROT sends to <CopyText>127.0.0.1</CopyText> on port <CopyText>9000</CopyText>, which is also VRChat's default, so you usually don't need to change it. To change it, use "OSC" under Settings → "Advanced".

## 3. Choose your languages

In "Language settings" on the left of the main screen, choose the two below. Translation goes both ways between them.

- "Your language" is the language you speak or type. What others say is translated into it.
- "Target language" is the language others speak or write. What you say is translated into it. You can choose up to three.

"Switch languages" between the two swaps them.

## 4. Turn on Translation and send

Turn on "Translation" on the left of the main screen. Until the translation model is downloaded, the Translation switch cannot be turned on, so download the model first.

- "Open setup" on the first screen lets you choose the speech recognition, translation and text to speech models for each feature and download them in one go.
- For the translation model alone, the icon beside the Translation switch ("Review the translation model download") downloads it too.

Either way, check the provider, license and size and agree before the download. When it finishes, turn on "Translation".

Setup does not open by itself. Until you finish it, the "Open setup" line on the first screen stands out. After "Done", the main screen's switches for the features you prepared ("Mic input" and "Listening", "Translation", "Text to speech (TTS)") are outlined in lilac until you turn them on. Setup never turns a switch on or changes a setting.

Type in the box at the bottom and send. The original and its translation appear in the log, and the same text appears in the VRChat chatbox.

::: tip Without a local model
To use an external translation API, enter its key under Settings → "Translation" → "More settings", then choose it under "Translator" on the main screen. See [Translation](../features/translation).
:::

"Before you start", which the main screen shows while the conversation log is empty, lists what is left to prepare. While speech recognition or translation is not ready, it shows "Open setup".

## 5. Choose your microphone and speaker

Do this before you use a voice feature (captions, text to speech, the voice changer). Open the settings with the gear at the bottom left of the main screen.

- For captions with "Local (speech.cpp)", choose the microphone and the game audio under "Speech recognition (ASR)" → "More settings" → "Realtime captions", in "Microphone input" and "Game audio output". At first they follow the system's defaults ([Speech recognition (ASR) and realtime captions](../features/transcription)).
- "Mic" and "Speaker" under "Device" are used by the cloud speech recognition engines. "Mic" is also where the voice changer's "Input to forward" starts. On Linux it lists PipeWire inputs.

"Auto select" follows the default microphone. On Windows, automatic speaker selection can also follow the output that is playing sound.

## 6. Show the features you use

At the top of the settings menu, "Used for" lets you choose how you use ASTAROT. The main screen's switches and the settings menu then show only the features those uses need.

| Use | What it is for |
|---|---|
| Basic | Your voice, recognized and translated, to the captions and the chatbox. A new install starts here |
| Nonverbal | Talk in text without speaking; read others' voices and chat |
| Multilingual conversation | Talk by voice with people of several languages |
| Voice changer (RVC) | Talk in a changed voice, with or without translation |
| Everything | Every feature and page. An update from an earlier version starts here |

- Pick as many as you like. What any of them needs is shown.
- Changing the uses turns no feature on or off and changes no setting. The switch of a feature that is on is shown whatever you pick.
- "Other features" under the switches shows the hidden ones. The settings search still finds hidden pages.
- Each settings page starts with what you need first. What you set once is under the page's "More settings".

The names of the uses may change after we hear from people who tried them.
