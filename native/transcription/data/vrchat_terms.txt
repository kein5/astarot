# The built-in VRChat vocabulary of the term dictionary (#446, #792). The app embeds this file
# when it is built (`speechcpp::vrchat_terms`); a term is added or its readings put right by
# editing this file, without touching the code.
#
# One term a line: `written=reading,reading`, the same form as the user's own terms and the
# settings' "add several terms" field. The readings may be left out (`ワールド`). Lines that start
# with `#` and blank lines are skipped.
#
# The rules of a user's term hold here too, and the tests check them
# (native/core/src/voice_settings.rs, `parse_term`):
# - the written form: at most 48 characters, without `,` or `=`;
# - at most 8 readings, each at most 32 characters, without `,` or `=`;
# - no written form twice, with letter case ignored.
#
# The first reading is the one the text to speech reads (#446).
